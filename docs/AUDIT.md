# Consolidated research and release audit

Final consolidation: 2026-09-05. Research and training are stopped. This
document supersedes older champion labels; it does not rewrite their artifacts.

Later input audit: [offline replay and assumption review](FINAL_INPUT_AUDIT.md)
reproduced complete action streams without a live environment and documented
a concrete cooldown-attribution error. It does not change the measured policy.

Packaging addendum: 2.0.1 adds user-requested, optional live viewing and GIF
playback-speed control. It does not alter any policy source or benchmark result.
See [viewer verification](VERIFICATION.md).

Repository addendum: 2.0.2 adds public-facing documentation, GitHub repository
scaffolding and the owner-approved MIT license. Historical verification records
remain unchanged; their former license-pending status is superseded by LICENSE.

## Central conclusion: what to ship

Ship **pocket**, one autonomous fixed symbolic controller, through
`crafter_symbolic.Agent()`. It is combined plus natural protected-crop
handling after diamond, not an ensemble or a seed-dependent selector.
Its frozen source's completed fresh stock evidence is **213/384 diamonds
(55.47%) and 69.98499 Crafter Score**. The optional combined reference has
368/640 diamonds (57.50%) and Score 68.72789 on a different pooled cohort.
See [full results, intervals and all achievements](BENCHMARKS.md).

Pocket is a practical recommendation for achievement breadth with the same
pre-diamond decision law. The data do **not** establish statistically significant
population superiority, optimality, or a solved survival problem. The existing
lab's corrected baseline and all retained policy sources remain unchanged;
choosing a release default does not retroactively alter their promotion record.

The shipping package has no perfect-night oracle, global map, hidden live
simulator channel, neural component, or dependence on the research directory.
No failed experimental composition, unfinished selector or ANN checkpoint is
enabled. This is release 2.0; historical release 1.0 remains an explicitly
privileged local-state baseline.

## Scope and limits of this audit

We reviewed the analytical and experimental record, observation/data contracts,
retained source and result provenance, completed fresh comparisons, late
research closures, neural histories, packaging dependencies and integration.
The portable [research inventory](../evidence/research_inventory.json) records
26 analysis documents and 52 neural training-history artifacts, with hashes.
The full detailed logs, bulky trajectories, models and old releases remain in
the lab; this repo contains the consolidated findings and portable benchmark
rows, not a duplicate of every research artifact.

This is a source/artifact audit with targeted executable verification, not
formal verification and not a replay of every historical episode. It cannot
guarantee zero perception errors or discover every future behavioral failure.
Only packaging tests were run during this consolidation; no improvement study
or ANN training was restarted.

## 1. Analytical foundation and original controller

The task was first treated as a dependency-constrained, partially observed
survival problem: find resources, craft progressively stronger pickaxes, obtain
diamond, then broaden achievements. The useful primitives are resumable
gather/craft/place/interact tasks, local pathfinding with relative odometry,
remembered resources and utilities, necessity service, shelter entry/repair,
combat/escape and directed frontier exploration.

The minimum diamond resource bill is five wood, five stone, one coal and one
iron. The corresponding non-travel action floor is 18; an early stone sword
adds one wood, one stone and a craft action, raising that floor to 21.
These are lower bounds under favorable adjacency, not realizable travel times
or a proof of optimal policy order. Orientation, route geometry, hazards,
resource reachability and food/water/sleep interrupt the nominal plan.

The most durable original choices were early stone-sword defense, remembered
diamond finishing, cheap extra-wood collection near the route, mountain-biased
frontiers, arrow/low-health guards, shelter revalidation and a delayed
radius-five exploration kernel after 300 unresolved post-pick actions. Symbolic
memory made discoveries persist across tool acquisition and survival detours.

The original v8 record mechanically pooled 892/1,600 diamonds (55.75%) over
four seed-stable courts, three involved in development. The reconstructed
v8-plus-score-v2 record scored 65.539 with 203/400 diamonds; a second court
scored 68.719 with 237/400. These were not all direct full-wrapper invocations:
some results were exact-row reconstructions and some finisher episodes stopped
after five target goals. More importantly, they used the old privileged
observation adapter. Preserve them as history; do not pool them with this
release or use them as corrected-contract human comparisons.

## 2. Human trajectories and first failed extensions

The official 100-game expert dataset helped identify long-term preparation,
tool use, food and exploration patterns. Achievement/action records remain
valid. Geometric diamond sightings and night enemy labels were not all
RGB-certified: seven of 33 first geometric diamond exposures were noisy.
The observation that nine of twelve successful games geometrically exposed
diamond before iron-pick acquisition is suggestive of a memory task, not a
certified count of human recognition events.

The resulting bounded searches were informative negatives, not omitted wins:

- Equal-cost surveys never intervened in a 40-case screen.
- Known-diamond component routing covered 649 eligible cases over five courts;
  iron-only routing changed 476 successes to 471, with broader routing worse.
- Four-step viability gating changed 12/58 exposed successes to 7.
  Two-view lookahead changed 3/10 hard cases to 2.
- Surplus-iron preparation never fired in 205 screened cases.
- Immediate unsealed night mining changed 619/1,055 eligible successes to 613;
  delayed/resealed mining changed 63/100 to 62.
- A plant-first wait on 40 already-solved cases reduced conditional Score from
  84.416 to 75.855. This was not a population benchmark.
- Small bound-20 versus bound-19 results were mixed; 19 was retained.

These are historical privileged-contract tests. Their mechanisms can inform
future hypotheses, but their rates are not clean release evidence. The first
resource-prior final-push collection was stopped before a canonical dataset,
model or held-out result and contributed no shipped policy.

## 3. Observation-faithful repair

The new perception front end matches exact RGB against legal public texture
combinations and known rendering transforms. It decodes the HUD, facing and
sleep appearance, and emits unknown when candidate evidence is ambiguous.
No live simulator fields are consulted by the decoder. Remembered static
terrain is not erased by missing nighttime evidence; unknown objects are not
declared absent.

Night acceptance uses fixed residual/margin thresholds, not calibrated
probabilities. Separate calibration worlds selected margins 0.12 for
materials, 1e-9 for absence and 0.02 for presence. The held-out eight-world,
127-frame audit observed zero errors among 6,951 accepted material labels,
7,088 accepted absence labels and 220 accepted presence labels. Coverage was
88.28%, 92.62% and 99.55% respectively; the class denominators differ.
All 127 tested player-facing/sleep labels were correct. Rare classes and future
frames are not exhaustively certified.

The corrected frozen baseline then obtained 207/400 diamonds (51.75%),
Score 66.7744, on fresh stock worlds. This establishes useful RGB-grounded
performance. Similarity to old scores on different worlds does not establish
that the original night leak was immaterial or validate old comparisons.

The autonomous interface also assumes that its proposed action is executed
once before the next observation. Teleports, forced inventories and action
overrides are outside this contract. That distinction matters for DAgger.

## 4. Neural translation: substantial work, not a replica

The intended inference contract was all-neural: RGB, previous action/reward,
learned recurrent state and action logits; no symbolic map, external planner
or teacher at inference. Prototypes used a tile/HUD-aware encoder and initially
two 512-unit LSTM layers, roughly 9.8 million parameters, with alternative
state-space and relative-transformer memory experiments. MPS was used on the
24-GB Apple-silicon machine.

The retained record includes behavior cloning, DART-style perturbations,
DAgger and teacher-assisted bridge collection, whole-network retraining,
symmetries, rare-action replay, episode/transition mixtures, correction
adapters, landmark probes, task/route/semantic/planner auxiliary supervision,
option heads and teacher-anchored PPO probes. Auxiliary labels were training
targets, not intended inference-time symbolic inputs.

Perception and offline policy accuracy improved substantially (roughly
70–83% action accuracy in several held-out trace evaluations), but autonomous
control remained poor. The protected v3 ANN evaluation obtained **0/384
diamonds and Score 5.652975**; it placed tables in 160 episodes, made wood
pickaxes in 154 and stone pickaxes in 30, and collected iron in six.
Mean lifespan was about 231.5 actions, all episodes ending by 440.
Teacher-assisted diamonds are not autonomous learner diamonds.

The machine-readable inventory contains 52 histories, including later rounds
17–42 beyond an earlier narrative checkpoint. Relative-transformer round 16
completed ten epochs and round 17 its five-epoch adapter; later rounds explored
task/route/semantic corrections and PPO. Round 41's option probe completed
three epochs; round 42's option policy stopped after two of four planned
epochs. Most late adapter studies froze the recurrent core. Their failure does
not rule out better trained long memory, and more architectural complexity is
not itself a demonstrated remedy.

The central unresolved contract risk is teacher state under learner action
overrides: some task flags can advance when the teacher proposes an action,
before knowing what was actually executed. The autonomous release does not
exercise that mismatch, but a future collector needs explicit executed-action
reconciliation. It is a plausible contributor, not a proven explanation for
all neural failure.

Pinned, not launched: audit/reconcile actual actions, then train the recurrent
controller on contiguous learner-control horizons (8/16/32/64) around useful
milestones, measuring closed-loop completion before another architecture
sweep. The ANN remains paused; no weights or neural dependencies are shipped.

## 5. Corrected symbolic opportunities and expedition search

The corrected opportunity series completed 672 comparison games. A fixture
proved that an inherited food-priority bug could ignore a visible ripe plant
while hungry. A bounded service change was retained. An apparent ore-tour
benefit reduced to a simpler cheap valid shared forge destination, so the
tour machinery was not retained. The narrow combat pilot required a stock
update-order correction for killed-object occupancy, followed by 600
single-/two-zombie transition checks.

Combined joined these narrow changes. It improved development point estimates
and scored 66.9775 with 70/128 diamonds on its first fresh court, versus
65.3965 and 64/128 for baseline; uncertainty included regression.

The later expedition series records **3,656 complete comparative episodes**,
with one earlier aborted row excluded and diagnostic/prefix/assisted replays
counted separately. Do not add overlapping pooled reports to manufacture a
larger independent sample or an exact total training-interaction budget.

| Family | What was actually tested | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Provisioning and exploration | Fifteen first-round policies over 1,408 development/selection episodes: provisioning, expeditions, joint logistics, substrate/adaptive exploration and persistent project agendas | Broad changes mostly regressed; natural post-diamond pocket isolated |
| Full composition | Mixed/stress combat and shelter changes plus selected components | 39/64 diamonds and Score 71.9059 in development did not hold up: fresh stock 138/256, 69.1191 versus combined 157/256, 69.8582 |

Rollout and combat models are approximate forward models of recognized local
state, not clones of the hidden simulator. Their fixed internal random tapes
do not read game RNG. Sampled local survival is not an invulnerability guarantee.

## 6. Final breadth audits: crops, moving food, investment

Construction was treated as geometry plus scheduling and supply, not merely
another planting threshold. Six executable constructor primitives passed
stock room/harvest fixtures. Sixty-four neutral pocket replays were all
action-hash identical: 38 diamond worlds, 32 geometries, eight affordable
cases, three plans. Eight clock/supply prefix replays were repeated after a
food-clamp-order correction. Only one viable harvest was predicted, already
harvested by baseline. No new full construction controller was promoted.
This screens those opportunities, not all conceivable farming strategies.

One plant provides four food per 301 growth updates. Growth, protection,
bootstrap food/water and active-update time matter; one crop is not indefinite
sustenance even while sleeping. In these traces there was no free pre-diamond
held-sapling/idle-farming opportunity to exploit.

Moving food prompted a different representation from static map coverage.
Three variants completed 192 development games: negative evidence scored
67.4271 with 35/64 diamonds; negative-plus-belief 69.0995 with 38/64;
pure belief 69.6884 with 38/64; pocket reference 70.6207 with 38/64.
A recently empty cow location can still be a useful viewpoint: in three lost
worlds a cow reappeared the next action. Diffusion/missing-state and predicted
view models improved a local proxy but not complete-episode Score. Closed.

Whole-return investment tested a small interpretable rule for buying an iron
sword after iron pick when no diamond was known and a legal forge/resource
opportunity existed. The pilot ran two arms on 128 fresh worlds (256 games),
plus eight smoke episodes. Never-invest obtained 73 diamonds and Score
69.6995; always-invest 72 and 71.3830. Score difference +1.6835 had interval
[-0.3608, 3.9901]; diamond difference -0.7813 points had interval
[-4.6875, 3.1250].

Only 18 worlds were eligible, below the prespecified minimum of 32.
The fitter correctly returned constant NEVER with insufficient support,
not a successfully learned selector. Validation 208 and stock 209 remained
unopened. The 19-value resource burden is a distance proxy, not a 19-action
duration guarantee: observed purchases took 14–249 elapsed actions, median 25,
including survival interruptions.

A new fixed larger replication began on fresh seed base 210000000 and was
stopped at the user's request after **416/512** complete JSONL rows.
Its partial outcomes were not inspected. No pooled selector was fitted;
validation 211 and stock 212 were not opened. Those rows and source archives
are preserved in the lab, excluded from public performance claims, and must
not be resumed without a new request.

## 7. Where the agent is still losing

Fresh combined batch 201 contained 243 diamond-positive worlds among 256.
The agent collected 157 diamonds. Of 86 positive-world failures, 41 occurred
before iron pick, 42 after iron pick without a registered diamond, and three
after iron pick with a registered diamond. There were 175 registered-diamond
episodes overall, with 157 conversions (89.71%). The first category can include
earlier sightings, so the final three are not all seen-but-failed episodes.
No odometry mismatch or geometric-ever-in-crop-but-never-registered cohort
appeared in that specific diagnostic court.

This suggests two large remaining losses: staying viable through the resource
chain and exploring productively after tool acquisition. Once a diamond is
both known and reachable with the tool, conversion is comparatively strong.
All pooled release episodes nevertheless died before 10,000 actions. Survival
is far from solved, but slower play, extra equipment and longer planning have
repeatedly changed exploration/combat timing adversely.

The old 10,000-world presence estimate was 9,394/10,000 (93.94%, Wilson
95% 93.46–94.39). Presence is only a loose environmental ceiling, not an
achievable rate for a local-view policy. The older 93.80% staged resource
bound was invalid because it demanded resources too early. A corrected
necessary relaxation admits all 243 positive worlds in batch 201 and supplies
no tighter ceiling there. Neither the attainable optimum nor the optimal
coverage/speed distribution has been established.

## 8. What we learned, and what remains pinned

The strongest general lesson is that **better local predictions are not the
same as better long-run action choices**. A correct six-step model, better
food belief or accurate resource prior can still choose expensive detours.
Task continuation, competing survival needs and opportunity cost matter.
Longer horizon alone is not an identified universal fix.

Achievements are irreversible episode events; food is a renewable service
requiring time and protection. A cheap one-time missing achievement may improve
geometric Score, but only if it does not destroy several other probabilities.
Pocket's post-diamond barrier protects acquisition policy; it cannot guarantee
a better remaining achievement profile.

The useful search discipline was to archive failures, isolate mechanisms,
require trigger support and whole-episode outcomes, freeze before fresh stock,
and reject selection winners when stock disagreed. New representations were
worth testing; there is no requirement that all progress be a monotone patch.
There is also no evidence that endless tiny threshold changes will close the
remaining gap.

If explicitly resumed, the most defensible symbolic research questions are
task-committed resource/survival planning with whole-return evaluation and
uncertainty-aware exploration whose cost is measured in complete episodes.
The investment replication is unfinished evidence, not a ready selector.
ANN work should resolve executed-action consistency and closed-loop milestone
control before claiming that more memory or PPO will solve imitation.

Nothing in this section authorizes or schedules more work. The current deliverable
is the frozen release, with tested integration, transparent scores and this audit.

## 9. Packaging disposition

Eight frozen policy modules were transplanted with package-relative imports;
normalized syntax trees match the originals. Only action/configuration
constants were extracted from the old adapter module. All 18 retained lab
hashes were checked before export. An integer-action facade, stock runner,
tests and docs were added; no gameplay tuning was performed.

The fresh installed Crafter matches the research installation in all 67 checked
Python, YAML and PNG files. The package runs without the lab, torch, MPS, API
keys or a neural checkpoint. Six complete episodes compared original versus
packaged actions on identical RGB/reward streams: all **3,870 actions** matched.
See [verification](VERIFICATION.md) for exact test/build status.

The folder is repo-ready and installs Crafter automatically. Nothing was
published remotely. The project owner subsequently approved MIT licensing for
2.0.2; third-party dependencies retain their own licenses. Detailed historical
artifacts and paused work stay intact in the lab.
